# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are trying their best with the information they had.

## What that means here

This project attracts two groups who are easy to be condescending towards: people
preparing for interviews they are nervous about, and people whose interviewing experience
does not match yours. Neither deserves it.

So, concretely:

- **No gatekeeping.** "You should already know this" is never a useful sentence in an
  issue. Someone asking a basic question is someone who trusted this project enough to
  ask.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** "This metric is wrong because X" is welcome
  and wanted. "You clearly have never interviewed anyone" is not.
- **Different interview experiences are all valid data.** Hiring bars differ by company,
  team, country and year. If someone's account does not match yours, that is interesting,
  not a lie.
- **No harassment of any kind**, including anything based on gender, gender identity,
  sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion,
  nationality or experience level.
- **Respect people's privacy.** Sessions contain someone's voice and their unpolished
  thinking. Never ask for audio, never share what someone posts here outside the thread.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to **ostap.belei@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and you will not be
asked to confront the other person.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, and block accounts, when this document is
not being followed. Where there is doubt, the comfort of the person on the receiving end
comes first.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
trimmed to what a project this size actually needs.
